Before you ask

Is this financial advice?

No. Every number in BYDL is an estimate: a starting point for your own planning, not a personal recommendation to save, spend, borrow, invest or move. BYDL is not a financial adviser and is not regulated as one. See the Financial Disclaimer.

How does the price estimate work?

You answer a few questions about the life you want: country, home, car, style, daily rhythm, travel. BYDL sends that as text to an AI model that estimates realistic, itemised monthly costs for that lifestyle in that place. AI can be wrong or out of date, so treat every figure as a starting point.

What does Premium include?

Every line of your monthly cost estimate, a lighter version of the same plan with what to change to reach it, your life plan (a step-by-step journey and a checklist), spending tips tied to your own choices, and unlimited plans.

Choose weekly, monthly or yearly; yearly works out cheapest. Your exact price in local currency is shown by the store before you confirm.

Is my data safe?

The BYDL app has no user accounts, sign-in or profile. It never asks for your email, phone number or any Apple or Google identifier. Your answers are sent once to generate your estimate and are not stored on our server afterwards. No ad SDKs and no tracking across other apps. Full details in the Privacy Policy.

How do I cancel a subscription?

On iPhone or iPad: Settings, your name, Subscriptions, BYDL, Cancel. On Android: Play Store, profile, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions, BYDL, Cancel. Cancelling stops the next charge; you keep Premium until the end of the period you already paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Which countries are supported?

BYDL prices lifestyles in over a hundred countries across every region, with more detail added as we grow.
