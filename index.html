Questions

Before you ask

Is this financial advice?

No. Every number in BYDL is an estimate built from current market data — a starting point for your own planning, not a personal recommendation to save, spend, borrow, invest, or move. See our Financial Disclaimer for the full picture.

How does the price estimate work?

You answer a few honest questions about the life you want — country, home, car, style, daily rhythm, travel. BYDL sends that to an AI model that estimates realistic, itemized monthly costs for that exact lifestyle in that exact place.

What does Premium include?

The full line-by-line cost breakdown, a lighter version of the same plan with what to change to reach it, your six-step path with monthly targets, and spending tips tied to your own choices.

Is my data safe?

BYDL has no user accounts, sign-in, or profile. Your answers are sent once to generate your estimate and are not stored afterwards. No ad SDKs, no tracking. Full details in our Privacy Policy.

Which countries are supported?

BYDL prices lifestyles in over a hundred countries across every region, with more detail being added as we grow.
